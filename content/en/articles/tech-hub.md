# What Makes a Place Perfect for Software Engineers?

Before we jet-set across the world, let’s break down what matters most to me as a software engineer:

- **Safety**: I want to code in peace, not dodge chaos.
- **Economy**: A strong tech scene means more gigs and growth.
- **Salary**: Let’s be real—those sweet, sweet euros, dollars, or whatever currency I fancy.
- **Health**: Good healthcare keeps me debugging life, not my body.
- **Legal/Visa Stuff**: Smooth sailing for work permits or residency (especially since I’m Latvian and EU-bound).

![preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vdeksne/portfolio-images-2025/refs/heads/main/traveler.png)

Now, buckle up—we’re exploring some top-tier destinations that I’ve been eyeing, including the Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Italy, and Spain!

## 1. Riga, Latvia - Home Sweet Home with a Tech Twist

Let’s kick things off with Riga, the Baltic gem that’s quietly turning into a tech hotspot. Since I’m from Latvia, staying or returning here is always on the table. Here’s the scoop:

- **Safety**: Riga’s pretty chill—homicide rates are around 3.7 per 100,000 (higher than the OECD average of 2.6, but manageable). I just stick to well-lit areas, and I’m good.
- **Economy**: Latvia’s ICT sector employs over 40,000 people and is growing fast. Startups like Printful and Lokalise are snapping up engineers like me.
- **Salary**: The average gross salary for software engineers hit €2,775 in 2020, and it’s climbing. After taxes (about 31% total, including social contributions), I’d pocket €1,900+ net monthly.
- **Health**: State-funded healthcare is solid if I’m employed, though private insurance (€20-50/month) gets me faster service. It’s not Europe’s best, but it’s getting better.
- **Legal/Visa**: As a Latvian, I’m an EU citizen—no visa hassles here or anywhere in the EU. Total freedom!
- **Why It’s Fun**: Riga’s medieval Old Town, affordable craft beer (€3/pint), and buzzing startup scene are hard to beat. Free lunch at some tech offices? Count me in!

**Pro Tip**: Living frugally, €700-1,000/month covers rent, food, and fun—plenty left for a new gaming rig.

## 2. Berlin, Germany - The Tech Titan of Europe

Next up: Berlin, where hipsters and coders collide in a tech symphony.

- **Safety**: Super safe—Germany’s crime rates are low, and Berlin’s vibe is welcoming (just gotta watch my bike!).
- **Economy**: Berlin’s a startup mecca—think N26, Zalando, and SoundCloud. The tech sector’s booming, and they’re desperate for engineers like me.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €50,000-€70,000 annually (pre-tax). After progressive taxes (30-40%), I’d net €3,000-4,000/month.
- **Health**: Top-notch public healthcare—mandatory insurance costs €150-200/month, but it’s worth it.
- **Legal/Visa**: As a Latvian, no visa needed—just register if I stay past 90 days.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Techno clubs, currywurst, and a multicultural scene that sparks app ideas. Rents (€800-1,200 for a 1-bedroom) are decent for a big city.

**Pro Tip**: Learning German could score me extra points, though English works in tech hubs.

## 3. Tallinn, Estonia - The Digital Nomad’s Dream

A quick hop from Latvia, Tallinn’s got my attention.

- **Safety**: Estonia’s one of Europe’s safest—low crime and a stable government.
- **Economy**: Home to Skype and Wise, Estonia’s tech scene is small but mighty. Nearly 150,000 IT pros work here, and the e-government vibe is unreal.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €30,000-€50,000/year. With a flat 20% tax, I’d keep €2,000-3,300/month net.
- **Health**: Solid healthcare—public insurance is €50-100/month, and quality’s decent.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen? No visa drama—just register for long-term stays.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Medieval charm meets Wi-Fi everywhere. Estonia’s crypto-friendly—maybe I’ll code a blockchain side gig!

**Pro Tip**: Living costs are low (€600-900/month), perfect for saving or startup cash.

## 4. Austin, Texas, USA - The Lone Star Tech Star

Crossing the pond to Austin, where BBQ and code rule.

- **Safety**: Generally safe, though property crime’s higher than violent crime. A good neighborhood like Mueller keeps me set.
- **Economy**: Austin’s a tech boomtown—Dell, Tesla, and Google are here. Jobs everywhere!
- **Salary**: Engineers earn $80,000-120,000/year. No state income tax in Texas means I’d net $5,000-8,000/month after federal taxes.
- **Health**: Private healthcare’s the norm—$300-500/month for insurance, but it’s top-tier.
- **Legal/Visa**: As a Latvian, I’d need an H-1B visa (tough but doable with a sponsor). It takes months and costs $1,500-3,000.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Live music, food trucks, and a chill vibe. South by Southwest (SXSW) is a coder’s dream!

**Pro Tip**: Rent’s $1,200-2,000 for a 1-bedroom, but the salary makes it worth the visa grind.

## 5. Lisbon, Portugal - Sunshine and Software

Lisbon’s where I could code by the coast.

- **Safety**: Super safe—low crime and friendly locals.
- **Economy**: A growing tech hub with startups like OutSystems and Feedzai, plus tax perks for foreign talent.
- **Salary**: €30,000-50,000/year. Taxes (20-30%) leave me with €2,000-3,300/month net.
- **Health**: Affordable public healthcare (€50-100/month), with solid private options.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen—no visa needed, just register after 90 days.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Pastéis de nata, beaches, and a laid-back lifestyle. Perfect work-life balance!

**Pro Tip**: Living costs are €800-1,200/month—cheap for Western Europe. Surf’s up after work!

## 6. Amsterdam, Netherlands - Canals and Code

Amsterdam’s got canals, bikes, and a tech scene that’s calling my name.

- **Safety**: Very safe—low crime rates and a relaxed vibe.
- **Economy**: A tech powerhouse with companies like Booking.com and Adyen. The demand for engineers is huge.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €50,000-70,000/year. After taxes (30-40%, or less with the 30% ruling for expats), I’d net €3,000-4,500/month.
- **Health**: Excellent healthcare—mandatory insurance is €100-150/month.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen—no visa, just register for long stays.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Biking everywhere, vibrant nightlife, and a global crowd. Plus, tulips!

**Pro Tip**: Rent’s €1,000-1,500 for a 1-bedroom, but the 30% tax break for five years is a game-changer.

## 7. Stockholm, Sweden - Nordic Cool Meets Tech

Stockholm’s got that Scandinavian charm I can’t ignore.

- **Safety**: Ultra-safe—Sweden’s crime rates are low, and it’s super stable.
- **Economy**: Home to Spotify and Klarna, Sweden’s tech sector is thriving with a focus on innovation.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €40,000-60,000/year. High taxes (30-50%) mean €2,500-3,500/month net, but benefits balance it out.
- **Health**: Free public healthcare—top-notch and covered by taxes.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen—no visa, just register.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Clean streets, fika (coffee breaks), and epic parental leave if I start a family.

**Pro Tip**: Living costs are €1,000-1,500/month—high taxes, but free healthcare and education make up for it.

## 8. Copenhagen, Denmark - Happiness and High Tech

Copenhagen’s all about that Danish hygge and tech opportunities.

- **Safety**: One of the safest places—low crime and a happy vibe.
- **Economy**: Tech’s growing with firms like Unity and Trustpilot. Copenhagen’s a startup and innovation hub.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €50,000-70,000/year. Taxes (35-50%) leave me with €2,800-4,000/month net.
- **Health**: Free, high-quality healthcare via taxes—Denmark’s got me covered.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen—no visa, just register.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Biking, cozy cafes, and a 37-hour workweek. Plus, those Danish pastries!

**Pro Tip**: Living costs are €1,200-1,800/month—pricey, but the quality of life is unmatched.

## 9. Oslo, Norway - Nature Meets Nerd Life

Oslo’s where I could code surrounded by fjords.

- **Safety**: Super safe—Norway’s crime rates are tiny, and it’s politically solid.
- **Economy**: Tech’s on the rise with companies like Kahoot! and renewable energy firms needing engineers.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €50,000-80,000/year. High taxes (30-40%) mean €3,000-4,800/month net, but salaries stretch far.
- **Health**: Free public healthcare—world-class and tax-funded.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen—no visa, just register.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Hiking, skiing, and Northern Lights. Work-life balance is king with 25+ vacation days.

**Pro Tip**: Living costs are €1,200-1,800/month—high, but the salaries and nature make it worth it.

## 10. Milan, Italy - Fashion, Food, and Code

Milan’s got style and a surprising tech edge.

- **Safety**: Pretty safe—moderate crime rates, but nothing wild in the tech areas.
- **Economy**: Italy’s tech scene is growing, with Milan hosting firms like Nexi and startups in fintech.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €30,000-50,000/year. Taxes (25-40%) leave me with €2,000-3,300/month net.
- **Health**: Public healthcare’s good and cheap (€50-100/month), with private options available.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen—no visa, just register.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Pasta, espresso, and fashion. Milan’s got a lively urban buzz!

**Pro Tip**: Living costs are €900-1,400/month—affordable for a chic city.

## 11. Barcelona, Spain - Mediterranean Magic

Barcelona’s sun, sea, and software make it tempting.

- **Safety**: Very safe—low violent crime, though pickpocketing’s a thing to watch.
- **Economy**: A rising tech hub with companies like Glovo and HP. Startups are popping up fast.
- **Salary**: Engineers earn €30,000-50,000/year. Taxes (20-30%) mean €2,000-3,300/month net.
- **Health**: Solid public healthcare (€50-100/month), plus affordable private options.
- **Legal/Visa**: EU citizen—no visa, just register.
- **Why It’s Fun**: Gaudi architecture, tapas, and beaches. Work hard, siesta harder!

**Pro Tip**: Living costs are €800-1,200/month—great value for a coastal city.

## Latvia-Specific Perks: Why My Passport Rocks

As a Latvian, EU citizenship is my golden ticket. No visa headaches in Europe—I can bounce between Riga, Berlin, Amsterdam, Stockholm, Copenhagen, Oslo, Milan, Barcelona, and Lisbon hassle-free. The US (Austin) is the outlier—H-1B visa’s a grind, but those salaries tempt me. Back home, Latvia’s low cost of living (€620-1,000/month) and growing tech scene keep it in the mix.

## Final Thoughts: Where Will I Code My Future?

So, where’s my next gig? Riga’s got roots and a rising tech pulse. Berlin’s the big leagues with cool vibes. Tallinn’s a digital utopia on a budget. Austin’s high-stakes, high-reward. Lisbon’s sunshine and balance. Amsterdam’s innovative and bike-friendly. Stockholm’s chill and progressive. Copenhagen’s happy and efficient. Oslo’s nature and tech combo. Milan’s stylish and affordable. Barcelona’s got that Mediterranean flair. Each spot mixes safety, economy, salary, health, and legal ease differently—I just need to pick what vibes with me.
